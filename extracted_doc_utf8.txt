1. Executive Summary
This document outlines a complete corporate carpool platform built for Karachi commuters. The platform connects office employees travelling on overlapping routes, enabling them to share rides, split fuel costs fairly, and build a trusted commuter community.
With petrol at Rs. 409.78 per litre as of 16 May 2026 (OGRA notification), a Karachi commuter driving 20 km each way burns roughly Rs. 820 per day in fuel alone. Our model cuts that to Rs. 210 per seat for riders and lets drivers actually earn Rs. 220 net after platform fee — turning the daily commute from a cost centre into a near break-even activity for drivers and a 75% savings for riders.

2. The Problem We Solve
Karachi has one of the worst commuter problems in South Asia. The combination of high fuel prices, traffic congestion, and limited safe public transport pushes employees toward expensive options that drain their salaries.
Pain Points
Fuel burn: 
An employee commuting 40 km round-trip daily spends Rs. 14,000 to Rs. 18,000 per month on petrol alone
Ride-hailing cost: 
Careem and inDrive for the same commute cost Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 35,000 per month
Safety concerns: 
Female employees often pay extra for trusted drivers or face harassment in public transport
Empty seats: 
Most cars on Karachi roads carry only the driver, while colleagues from the same office area drive separately
No trust layer: 
Existing carpool attempts (WhatsApp groups, Facebook) lack identity verification, payment safety, and accountability
Why Now
Petrol prices have crossed Rs. 400 four times in 2026. Corporate HR teams are actively looking for employee retention perks. Smartphone penetration in Karachi white-collar workforce is above 95%. The market is ready.
3. The Solution
A closed-community carpool platform where employees verified through their company email can offer or request rides. Pricing is locked to the live OGRA fuel rate so it is always fair and transparent. Payments flow through the app, eliminating awkward cash settlements. Every ride builds a reputation score.
Core Value Propositions
For Riders: 
Save 75% vs Careem. Save 50% vs driving alone. Verified colleagues only.
For Drivers: 
Recover your full fuel cost and earn a small profit. Make new friends from your sector.
For Companies: 
Offer a real perk that reduces parking pressure, late arrivals, and employee stress. Optional subsidy.

5. Complete User Workflow
This is the full journey from first download to repeat ride. Each step is designed to remove friction and build trust.
5.1 Onboarding (First 90 Seconds)
User downloads the app from Play Store / App Store, or signs up on the web.
Enters phone number — receives OTP via SMS.
Enters corporate email address (e.g. name@company.com). System sends verification link to confirm employment.
Profile setup: full name, profile photo, gender, home area, office area, working hours.
Identity verification: uploads CNIC front + back. Auto-OCR check + manual review within 4 hours.
Chooses role: Rider, Driver, or Both. Drivers add vehicle details, registration number, license photo.
Onboarding ends with a 30-second route preference setup — preferred departure window, music preference, smoking yes/no, women-only ride preference.
5.2 Posting a Ride (Driver Flow)
Driver taps 'Offer a Ride' on home screen.
Picks From and To locations using map pin or saved Home / Office shortcuts.
Selects departure date, time, number of available seats (1-4).
App auto-calculates fair price per seat based on distance × current OGRA petrol rate ÷ 6 km/L ÷ seats + small driver margin.
Driver can accept the suggested price or set custom (within ±15%).
Adds optional notes: 'AC car, no smoking, female passengers welcome'.
Hits 'Publish'. Ride goes live for matching riders.
5.3 Finding a Ride (Rider Flow)
Rider opens 'Find a Ride' tab.
App auto-fills home → office route from saved preferences.
Shows matching rides sorted by: closest pickup, departure time match, driver rating, price.
Each ride card displays: driver photo, name, company, rating, vehicle, total ride price, your seat price, pickup walk distance, departure ETA.
Rider taps 'Request Seat'. Optionally adds a short message to driver.
Driver gets a push notification with rider profile and can Accept or Decline within 15 minutes.
On Accept, both get a confirmation screen with ride details, in-app chat unlocked, pickup point shared on map.
5.4 Day of Ride
1 hour before departure, both get a reminder push.
Driver taps 'Start Ride' when leaving home. Live location shared with rider.
Rider sees driver's car icon approaching on map, with live ETA.
On arrival, driver taps 'Picked Up' and rider confirms with in-app code (4 digits) — prevents fraud and confirms identity.
During ride, live tracking shared with one emergency contact of rider for safety.
On arrival at destination, both tap 'Complete Ride'. Payment auto-deducts from rider's wallet to driver's wallet, minus 10% platform fee.
5.5 Post-Ride
Both parties rate each other 1-5 stars with optional comment.
Receipt emailed automatically with fuel cost breakdown.
Rider can tap 'Repeat this ride' to schedule the same route for next day or weekly.
Driver's earnings are withdrawable to bank account every Friday (minimum Rs. 1,000).

7. Pricing Model — Karachi Fuel-Based Formula
Pricing is the heart of this product. Every other carpool app fails because the price feels arbitrary. Ours is anchored to the live OGRA petrol rate, so users see exactly why they are paying what they are paying.
The Formula
Rider Seat Price = (Distance km ÷ 6 km/L) × Petrol Rate ÷ Seats + Rs. 8/km Driver Margin
Assumptions used in calculation:
Petrol rate: 
Rs. 409.78 per litre (OGRA notification, 16 May 2026)
Average sedan efficiency: 
6 km per litre in Karachi traffic
Default seats: 
3 riders sharing
Driver margin: 
Rs. 8 per km — covers car depreciation, oil, tyre wear
Platform commission: 
10% of rider payment

Dynamic Pricing Logic
Petrol price is fetched daily from a scraped OGRA notification source. When OGRA updates rates (every 1st and 16th of the month), our pricing engine recalculates all future rides automatically. Users are notified if their saved routes change in price by more than Rs. 10.

Stack
Mobile: 
Flutter (single codebase for iOS + Android)
Web: 
Next.js 14 with App Router, Tailwind CSS

Node.js + Express + TypeScript

PostgreSQL via Supabase (auth, realtime channels, storage)

Google Maps Platform (Directions, Distance Matrix, Places)

JazzCash + EasyPaisa integration via PayFast or Safepay aggregator. Bank transfers via 1LINK for driver payouts.
Realtime chat: 
Supabase Realtime channels

Firebase Cloud Messaging
Fuel price scraper: 
Cron job fetching OGRA notification daily, writing to database

AWS (EC2 + RDS for backend, Vercel for Next.js frontend)
Monitoring: 
Sentry for errors, Mixpanel for product analytics

Every piece is battle-tested by Developers of Chicago across previous projects — Swifpack logistics platform (Tanzania Posts), Smart Pump ERP, NGO Assist. Flutter and Node.js give us speed without sacrificing performance. Supabase removes 60% of backend boilerplate, letting us ship the MVP in 12 weeks instead of 20.

13. Risks & Mitigation
Driver liability accidents: 
Partner with EFU or Adamjee for in-ride insurance bundled at Rs. 5 per ride
Payment fraud: 
All payments held in escrow by platform until ride completion confirmed by both
Female safety: 
Women-only mode, SOS button, live tracking, mandatory CNIC + driver license verification
Petrol price volatility: 
Pricing auto-updates with OGRA — users always pay fair rate
Slow adoption: 
Corporate pilots de-risk launch by guaranteeing initial user base
Regulatory: 
Engage early with PTA and Sindh Excise — position as community service, not ride-hailing
